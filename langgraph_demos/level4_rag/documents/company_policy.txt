ACME COMPANY POLICY

Paid Leave:
Every full-time employee receives 24 paid leave days per calendar year.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week. More than three remote days requires written manager approval.

Laptop Reimbursement:
The company reimburses approved laptop purchases up to INR 80,000.

International Travel:
All international business travel requires manager approval before tickets are purchased.

Food Expenses:
During approved business travel, food expenses are reimbursed up to INR 1,500 per day.

Training:
Employees can claim up to INR 30,000 per year for approved technical training and certifications.
