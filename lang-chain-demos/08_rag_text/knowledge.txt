Go concurrency uses goroutines and channels. A goroutine is a lightweight concurrent function execution managed by the Go runtime. Channels provide typed communication between goroutines. Unbuffered channels synchronize sender and receiver. Buffered channels can hold values up to their capacity. Context is commonly used to propagate cancellation and deadlines across goroutines and service boundaries. A worker pool bounds concurrency by using a fixed number of workers consuming jobs from a channel.
